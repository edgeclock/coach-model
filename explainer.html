Briefing for the Chairman, 13 Aug 2026

What is GBI, and what does it do to our business model?

You approved building both your own organization and Client #1's business on the Guerrilla Business Intensive framework. Here is what that actually means, in plain words, and exactly how it changes both businesses.

1 What GBI actually is

GBI stands for Guerrilla Business Intensive: a 4 to 5 day, high-energy live bootcamp created by T. Harv Eker (the author of Secrets of the Millionaire Mind) and run across Asia by his events company, Success Resources.

It is not a lecture series. It is a working bootcamp where attendees build a real business plan over several days, present it in front of the room, and get it torn apart and rebuilt by a panel of wealthy entrepreneurs and by their peers. The slogan of the program is: "stop wasting time with tactics that produce little or mediocre results and start creating wealth in any business you choose."

The 4 signature mechanics (the parts worth stealing)

Mechanic 1

Hot seat

One person presents their business plan to the room. An expert panel plus the audience dissects it live. Fast, public, unsparing. You either defend your plan or fix it on the spot.

Mechanic 2

Peer pods

Attendees group in threes or fours and walk each other through their plans, with the others analyzing and stress-testing. This is the workhorse mechanic: most people never reach the stage, the pod is where the plan gets real.

Mechanic 3

Live plan makeover

Your plan is not critiqued and left to die. It is reworked on the spot, and you leave with a rebuilt deliverable, not just notes.

Mechanic 4

Timeboxed intensity

4 to 5 straight days, long hours, deliberate pressure. Decisions get made because there is no time left to defer them.

2 What is inside the program (the 11 modules)

Success Resources does not publish an official syllabus, so this is reconstructed from the program's marketing, its 24-CD home study set, and attendee reviews. Directionally accurate, and it is the pattern we borrow, not a document we copy.

Module 1

Million-dollar idea generation

Rapid ideation drills, idea volume over precision, then filter. Learn to generate business ideas in minutes.

Module 2

Guerrilla financing

Raising capital without banks. Finding and pitching investors, using other people's money, starting with minimal investment.

Module 3

Business plan creation and makeover

Build a plan fast, then have it torn apart and rebuilt by faculty and peers until it holds.

Module 4

Maverick / guerrilla marketing

Low-cost, high-leverage, unconventional customer acquisition. Marketing on a budget that actually works.

Module 5

Internet marketing

Online lead generation, funnels, and selling services and products digitally.

Module 6

Negotiation and language

Real-world negotiation tactics, persuasion language, deal structuring.

Module 7

Competitive research

Systematically studying competitors before committing money to anything.

Module 8

Cheap top talent

How to get top personnel for half the cost: leverage, contractors, performance pay.

Module 9

Systemization

Building the business so it runs without the founder. The "automatic money machine."

Module 10

Sales strategy

Building a repeatable sales machine, not relying on being a natural closer.

Module 11

Build to sell

The signature thesis: a business is built to be sold, so you can build another and sell again. Design for exit from day one.

The order matters: idea generation, then competitive research, then offer design with minimal capital, then a plan draft, then a hot-seat teardown, then the makeover, then marketing and sales, then financing only if needed, then systemization, then exit design. The plan is written early and deliberately criticized before any money is spent.

3 What GBI does to YOUR business model

You are not copying GBI as a product. You are borrowing its method and its discipline and applying them to your real business: the coach network.

"The coach owns the business. Edge owns the method." That line is GBI thinking, applied.

Your frame, GBI style

  • Your "business to sell" is the network itself. Every GBI module maps to a build step for the organization.
  • Your product is business setup plus coaching. Your customers are professionals like Client #1.
  • Your financing is bootstrap: no investors, no loans. Your working capital is the members' collected revenue, paid to you as the 10% royalty, monthly in arrears.
  • Your systemization is the playbook: build the Business Setup Playbook once from Client #1, then every coach delivers the same playbook. You shift from delivery to certification.
  • Your build-to-sell metric is verified recurring royalty per coach, not coach count. A network with signed agreements, a royalty ledger and verified revenue reporting is saleable at a multiple of the recurring royalty stream.

The two funnels (do not blend them)

Funnel A

Client funnel

Professionals who want a business set up. Sell the outcome: "a real business in 90 days."

Funnel B

Coach funnel

Proven operators who want to replicate. Invitation only, fed exclusively from the client funnel: client becomes mentee, mentee becomes coach-in-training. Nobody joins as a recruiter; they graduate as an operator. This is your best anti-pyramid defence AND your best selection filter.

Your future product, GBI style: compress the playbook into your own branded 3-day Business Build Intensive. Day 1: idea, offer, validation. Day 2: plan build, peer pods, hot seats. Day 3: marketing, sales, funnel build, 90-day launch commitment. Run version 1 with Client #1 as an audience of one. Run version 2 with 6 to 10 people.

Force a niche, today

5 The legal lines (what we can and cannot touch)

Names: "Guerrilla Business Intensive", "Guerrilla Business School", "Quantum Leap" TRADEMARKED Do not use as our program name. No affiliation or endorsement claims, ever.
Manuals, workbooks, slides, CDs, scripts COPYRIGHTED No copying, no close paraphrasing. Rewrite in our own words with PH examples.
Nominative mention ("influenced by intensives like GBI") LOW RISK OK, minimal and factual. Never on a logo, headline or sales hero.

Read the actual document before building a commercial derivative. And run an IPOPHL trademark search on our own program name before printing anything.

6 Why this does not make us a pyramid (and the words we use)

GBI's revenue engine is event tickets and an escalating program ladder, with aggressive room-selling. That is the part we deliberately do not import. Our engine is: setup engagement, then a perpetual 10% royalty that only exists when the member actually collects revenue from real outside clients.

  • NO payment for recruiting anyone, ever.
  • NO entry fee (or nominal only). No inventory, no packages.
  • ONLY 2 generations deep, capped: trainer 5%, grand-trainer 1.5%, out of the single 10%.
  • MEMBERS own their own businesses and clients. A priced, contractual exit exists.

Vocabulary discipline (permanent)

Never: downline, upline, recruit, levels, passive income, financial freedom, ground floor. Use: license, network, coach, trainer-of-record, override, generation, royalty, mentorship override.

Your memorized one-paragraph answer: "I don't get paid to recruit anyone, there is no fee for joining and no payment for enrolling. I get a royalty only when a member actually collects money from their own clients, for their own business, which they own outright. If they never collect, I never earn."
